Diaphragm is the area below lungs. 
We are habitual of breathing via Ribcage and Stomach for so long that we fall short of breath when trying to speak something longer.
##### How to know if you are using Diaphragm?
Place the hand on Diaphragm to feel the vibration while speaking.
