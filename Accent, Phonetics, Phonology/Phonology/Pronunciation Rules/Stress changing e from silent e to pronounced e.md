[[Noun Stress]]
[[Adjective stress]]

- **mínute** (noun) = **60 seconds**
    - Stress is on the **first syllable**.
    - The silent **e** does **not** lengthen the **u**.
    - Pronounced **/ˈmɪnɪt/**.
- **minúte** (adjective) = **very small**
    - Stress is on the **second syllable**.
    - The silent **e** gives the **u** its usual long pronunciation (**/juː/** or **/uː/**).
    - Pronounced **/maɪˈnjuːt/** (BrE) or **/maɪˈnuːt/** (AmE).
