### Explanation
The vowels shifted their pronunciation by one place over several centuries, and this especially affected [[Long Vowel]]. This made it so that the long-vowel was no more merely the extended pronunciation of its short counterpart, but it was another sound altogether - specifically, taking the sound of long pronunciation of the sound next to it in the hieracrchy. Thus, every long-vowel sound was shifted, but the spelling was never updated to show the change.
