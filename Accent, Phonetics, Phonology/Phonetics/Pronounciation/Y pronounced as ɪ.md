[[KIT vowel ɪ]]

When a word is 
- Latin Derived
- Has Y in a stressed syllable
It is pronounced as ɪ often times.

*BUT*

When Y
- at the beginning of the word
- It is pronounced as /j/, and not /ɪ/

### Pronunciation of Y as [ɪ]

In certain situations, the letter Y is pronounced as [ɪ]. This occurs when:

* The word is derived from Latin
* The Y appears in a stressed syllable

In these cases, the Y is often pronounced as a short [ɪ] sound.

The following tables provide examples of words that exhibit this pronunciation pattern:

### Latin-Derived Words

| Word | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- |
| Gym | /ɡɪm/ |
| Lyric | /ˈlɪrɪk/ |
| Myth | /mɪθ/ |
| Typist | /ˈtɪpɪst/ |
| Lympic | /lɪmˈpɪk/ |

### Words with Y in a Stressed Syllable

| Word | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- |
| Myth | /mɪθ/ |
| Type | /taɪp/ (note: can also be pronounced as /tɪp/) |
| Lyric | /ˈlɪrɪk/ |
| Rhythm | /ˈrɪðəm/ |
| Hydro | /ˈhɪdroʊ/ |

### Words with Y in an Unstressed Syllable

| Word | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- |
| Happy | /ˈhæpi/ |
| Baby | /ˈbeɪbi/ |
| City | /ˈsɪti/ |
| Fancy | /ˈfænsi/ |
| Mummy | /ˈmʌmi/ |

### Exceptions to the Rule

| Word | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- |
| Yes | /jes/ |
| Yacht | /jɒt/ |
| Yule | /juːl/ |
| Yolk | /joʊk/ |
| Yonder | /ˈjɒndər/ |

These tables demonstrate the various ways in which the letter Y can be pronounced, including the [ɪ] sound in certain Latin-derived words and words with Y in a stressed syllable.
