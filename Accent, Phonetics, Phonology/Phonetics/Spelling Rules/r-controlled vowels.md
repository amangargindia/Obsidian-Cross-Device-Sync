An **r-controlled vowel** is a vowel followed by the letter **r**, where the **r** changes the vowel's usual pronunciation, creating a new vowel sound.

| Pattern | Example Words       | IPA (BrE) | IPA (AmE) |
| ------- | ------------------- | --------- | --------- |
| ar      | car, park, star     | /ɑː/      | /ɑr/      |
| er      | her, term, fern     | /ɜː/      | /ɝ/       |
| ir      | bird, first, girl   | /ɜː/      | /ɝ/       |
| ur      | turn, burn, nurse   | /ɜː/      | /ɝ/       |
| or      | fork, storm, horse  | /ɔː/      | /ɔr/      |
| ear     | learn, pearl, early | /ɜː/      | /ɝ/       |
| air     | fair, chair, hair   | /eə/      | /er/      |
| are     | care, share, square | /eə/      | /er/      |
| ear     | bear, wear, pear    | /eə/      | /er/      |
| ear     | hear, fear, near    | /ɪə/      | /ɪr/      |
