The ending of some words differs between [[British English]] and [[American English]]. In British English, these words typically end in "-t", while in American English, they end in "-ed". The following table and list illustrate these differences:

| British | American |
| ------- | -------- |
| Learnt  | Learned  |
| Spelt   | Spelled  |

Additional examples of this variation include:
burnt/burned, 
spoilt/spoiled, 
misspelt/misspelled, 
smelt/smelled, 
spilt/spilled, 
dwelt/dwelled, 
dreamt/dreamed, 
knelt/kneeled.
