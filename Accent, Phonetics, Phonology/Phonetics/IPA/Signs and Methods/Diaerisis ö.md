This use of the diaeresis peaked in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, but it was never applied extensively across the language: only a handful of diaereses, including _coöperation_ and _Brontë_, are encountered with any appreciable frequency in English; thus _reëxamine_, _reïterate_, _deëmphasize_, etc. are seldom encountered. In borrowings from Modern French, whose [orthography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_orthography "French orthography") utilizes the diaeresis as a means to differentiate [graphemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapheme "Grapheme"), various English dictionaries list the dieresis as optional (as in _naive_ and _naïve_) despite the juxtaposition of a and i
