### Use?
It is used to show that the adjacent vowel to another vowel is pronounced separately.
Usually, in English, we pronounce two [[Vowel]] written right next to each other as [[Diphthong]].
We use this mark (as in the one above e of Noël) to show that they are pronounced separately.
coöperate
reëlect
 _The New Yorker_ is one of the rare publications that still use the diaeresis sign.

#### Should we use it?
The short answer is no. The diaeresis is considered obsolete by most authorities in the English language. In some cases, the diaeresis is replaced by the hyphen. Where _The New Yorker_ writes coöperate, other publications prefer co-operate, especially outside the United States. In other cases, you’ll find it written simply as cooperate. People generally know how to pronounce these words.
