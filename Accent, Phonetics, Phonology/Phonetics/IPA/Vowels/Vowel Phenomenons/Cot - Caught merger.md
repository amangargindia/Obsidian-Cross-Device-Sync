![[Vowel Merger]]

In the cot–caught merger, the unrounded open back vowel [ɑ] (as in _cot_) and the rounded open-mid back vowel [ɔ] (as in _caught_) blend into a single vowel sound.

1. **The Unrounded Open Back Vowel [ɑ]**: This is the most common result in **General American** and **Canadian English**. Words like _cot_, _caught_, _bot_, _bought_, _Don_, and _Dawn_ are all pronounced with the open "ah" sound (like the vowel in _father_).
    
2. **The Open-Mid Back Vowel [ɒ] or [ɔ]**: In some specific regional dialects (like parts of Western Pennsylvania or Canada), the merged vowel leans slightly more rounded or raised, sounding closer to a short "aw".
