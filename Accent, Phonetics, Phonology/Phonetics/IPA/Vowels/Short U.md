### What is it?
> **Short ⟨u⟩ can variably represent either /ʊ/ or /ʌ/, as a result of the foot–strut split.**

Originally, the short **u** represented one vowel. Later, in many dialects (especially Southern British English), that vowel **split into two different vowels**.
This historical change is called the **FOOT–STRUT split**.

| Pronunciation     | Example Words                                              | Notes                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| /ʊ/ (FOOT vowel)  | put, pull, full, bull, bush, butcher, cushion, woman, wolf | A short, rounded vowel. Mainly found after the FOOT–STRUT split in words that retained the older /ʊ/ sound.                                       |
| /ʌ/ (STRUT vowel) | cut, cup, sun, luck, mud, run, much, nut, duck, under      | A short, central vowel. In Southern British English and most other dialects with the FOOT–STRUT split, many historical /ʊ/ vowels shifted to /ʌ/. |

#### The J sound 
The initial **/j/** ("y" sound) is **optional in many words**.

| Word     | Conservative British (RP)             | General American | Difference                                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| tune     | /tjuːn/                               | /tuːn/           | /j/ ("y" sound) dropped in American English     |

| Tuesday  | /ˈtjuːzdeɪ/                           | /ˈtuːzdeɪ/       | Yod-dropping after /t/                          |
| student  | /ˈstjuːdənt/                          | /ˈstuːdənt/      | Yod-dropping after /st/                         |
| duke     | /djuːk/                               | /duːk/           | Yod-dropping after /d/                          |
