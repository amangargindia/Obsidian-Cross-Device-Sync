## When does it occur?

In a **stressed [[Doubling rule - VCV pattern|Closed syllable]], the letter **a** is usually pronounced **/æ/** when:

- it is followed by one or more consonants, and
- it is **not part of another spelling pattern** (such as [[Silent e pattern|Silent E]], [[Vowel Teams]], [[r-controlled vowels]], etc.).

### Affected By
#### [[Vowel Teams]]
The pronunciation of **`a`** changes because, in a **vowel team**, it no longer represents its usual short sound (/æ/) by itself. Instead, it combines with another vowel letter to represent a **different vowel sound**.

| Vowel Team | Example Words | IPA | Resulting Sound |
|------------|---------------|-----|-----------------|
| ai | rain, train, paint | /eɪ/ | Long A (diphthong) |
| ay | day, play, stay | /eɪ/ | Long A (diphthong) |
| au | author, August, fault | /ɔː/ (BrE), /ɔ/ (AmE) | Broad "aw" vowel |
| aw* | saw, law, draw | /ɔː/ (BrE), /ɔ/ (AmE) | Broad "aw" vowel (*not a vowel team, since w is a consonant*) |
| ea | break, steak, great | /eɪ/ | Long A (irregular vowel team) |
| ea | head, bread, ready | /ɛ/ | Short E (irregular) |
| ea | sea, clean, speak | /iː/ | Long E (irregular) |
| oa | boat, road | /oʊ/ (AmE), /əʊ/ (BrE) | Long O |
