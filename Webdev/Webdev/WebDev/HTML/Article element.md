what is article element
? #AOSR/flnao/m/55nt
The `<article>` element is a semantic HTML5 container for self-contained, independently distributable content. It represents a piece of content that makes sense on its own, like a blog post, news story, forum comment, or product card. It often contains its own heading (`<h1>`–`<h6>`) and can include `<header>`, `<footer>`, and nested `<article>` elements. Use `<article>` when the content could be syndicated or reused standalone. For thematic grouping within a larger document, use `<section>` instead. Example:
```html
<article>
  <h2>Blog Post Title</h2>
  <p>This is a standalone piece of content.</p>
</article>
```
