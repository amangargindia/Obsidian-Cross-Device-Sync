#Q #AOSR/3e9vu
What is justify-content and align-items property
? #AOSR/3e9vu/m/z358
In CSS (primarily in **Flexbox** and **Grid**), **`justify-content`** aligns items along the **main axis** (controlling spacing between/around items), while **`align-items`** aligns items along the **cross axis** (controlling how individual items sit across the height or width of the container). [[1](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/css/difference-between-justify-content-vs-align-items-in-css/), [2](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Flexible_box_layout/Aligning_items)]

A simple way to remember the axes: [[1](https://css-tricks.com/a-quick-way-to-remember-difference-between-justify-content-align-items/), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0o0RF13I4-g&t=1)]

- **Justify** = Horizontally aligned (Row direction)
- **Align** = Vertically aligned (Column direction)  
    _(Note: These directions flip if your container uses `flex-direction: column`)_ [[1](https://css-tricks.com/a-quick-way-to-remember-difference-between-justify-content-align-items/), [2](https://medium.com/@kristinethejohnson/css-grid-the-confusion-of-align-justify-items-align-justify-content-and-align-justify-e94ac687fdb), [3](https://openclassrooms.com/en/courses/5295881-create-web-page-layouts-with-css/5415181-align-items-and-justify-content), [4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0o0RF13I4-g&t=1)]
