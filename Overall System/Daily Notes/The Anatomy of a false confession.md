I picked this book up as an audio book from [[Myanonamouse]], but decided I am good with [[Kindle]] only as I struggle to keep up with audiobooks nowadays.

It was a set of 15 real stories of how a person spent so many years in jail, and then evidence emerged and they were let go.

Horrible to think of, that still, there are so many innocent people doing time for something they did not do,
and to think that, may god not make it the case, I, or the next person - can also be wrongly convicted.

There was one thing in the book that you knew that the person will be released later so you did not had that anxiety of "what would happen", but still, the lost year, their dispair...

I mean how do you loose like 20-25 years of your life so casually and come out mentally stable? That is like my entire life and then some years.. It is so long of a time..

A funny despair takes over me as I think about that.. Like we get one life, in that one life, if half the time is taken over by a wrongful conviction, how must that feel, how horrible - wretched that must be!!

Plus how shamelessly the judiciary just admits that yeah okay we were wrong - the fuck are you going to do about it?
And the fucking time it takes? Why the hell it took you like years to just Deny a plea?

Man I hope me or like anyone does not falls in this trap....
