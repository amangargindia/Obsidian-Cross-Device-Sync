Gerund?
Verb ki -ing form ko ham usually continuous aspect me use karte hai. Iski madad se ham ongoing action batate hai
"I am working."
"I was working."
"I will be working."

Lekin, jab aap uss action ko kar nahi rahe ho, par uske baare me baat kar rahe ho - to kya?
"I like drinking water." - isme koi kaam to ho nahi raha, ek general 'fact' bataya hai, present simple tense hai..
"I like writing." - Koi cheez karna jo aapko pasand hai, par abhi ho nahi rahi hai...

to jab aap koi action kar nahi rahe ho, bas uske baare me baat kar rahe ho, to ham verb ka ing form noun ki tarah use karte hai.

Aur jab verb ka ing form noun ki tarah use hota hai, to ham ussey Gerund bolte hai.
