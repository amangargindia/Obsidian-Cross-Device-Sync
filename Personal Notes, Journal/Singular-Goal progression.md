Singular-Goal Progression was one of the main promises of [[The Great Reset of 2026]]. 
Under this plan, the ideation was to take one thing, master it, then move on to another, as opposed to the prevailing method of knowledge and skill acquisition of just randomly going about doing things - which led to things which were more interesting to take precedence over the ones which were more important.

The singular goal progression started with [[Book - English Phonetics and Phonology]]
The [[Skill Wishlist was thus born to keep track of things to do]].

The hope is that projects will not be abandoned, and that there would be more completion due to the compulsion of having to complete things rather than abandoning them.
