[WORD] Glimmer

[PHONETIC] GLIM-er

[DEFINITION] Halki si soft shine ya faint light.

[NARRATIVE] Imagine ek dark room mein ek mirror rakha hai. Suddenly usme se halki glowing light nikalti hai.

“Glow mirror… glimmer…”

Poora room dark hai but mirror softly shine kar raha hai.

Yahan “Glow mirror” sunne mein “Glimmer” jaisa lagta hai, aur tiny soft light directly meaning show karti hai.

[STORY] Valley raat ko terrace par baitha tha. Bijli chali gayi thi aur poori colony dark thi. Sirf aasman mein stars dikh rahe the. Suddenly usne saamne wali building ki balcony mein ek tiny light notice ki.

Woh light bahut weak thi, bas halki si shine kar rahi thi. Valley ne Deepmaala ko message kiya, “Udhar dekho, ek small **Glimmer** dikh raha hai.”

Deepmaala terrace par aayi aur dono us faint light ko dekhne lage. Pata chala kisi ne balcony mein tiny candle jalayi thi. Hawa chalne par uski light aur softly flicker karne lagi.

Valley bola, “Darkness mein itna chhota **Glimmer** bhi noticeable lagta hai.”

Thodi der baad moon clouds ke peeche se nikla aur poori terrace par silver light spread ho gayi.

[USAGE]

[Dark place mein halki si light dikh rahi ho | “A glimmer of light appeared in the tunnel.”]

[Hope ya positivity ka small sign ho | “There was still a glimmer of hope.”]

[Kisi shiny object par faint reflection ho | “The necklace gave a glimmer in the moonlight.”]

[Kisi ki aankhon mein excitement ki halki shine ho | “A glimmer appeared in her eyes.”]

[Distance se weak sparkle ya shine dikh rahi ho | “We saw a glimmer far away.”]
