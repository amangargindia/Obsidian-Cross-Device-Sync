## Procedure
1. Decide word or choose from [[Words for vocabpod]]
2. Make the script using [[Vocab generation prompt]]
3. Paste it using [[Weekly Vocabpod template N]]
4. Make images of them using Meta AI and screenshot them by properly naming it
5. Paste it into [PDF creator](https://fluencybridgeedu.online/tools/vocabpodgenerator.html
6. Record them all in Dolby recorder
7. Enhance them in [Adobe Podcases](https://podcast.adobe.com/en/enhance)
8. Edit audio in Instagram edits app to have Music, keep music at 20%
9. Upload and shcedule it on [Spotify Creators site ](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/show/6dkHRPEDxRLboNcw42uXXA/episode/wizard)
10. Backup audio and pdfs to [Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Ls5atdcPLgPeSSkb8p1xuE_yvt7GyRuC)
11. Upload and schedule it to YT, Linkedin
12. Share links on Insta, WhatsApp and Discord
