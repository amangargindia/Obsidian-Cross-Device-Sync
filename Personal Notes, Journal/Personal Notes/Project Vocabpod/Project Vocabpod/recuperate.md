[WORD] Recuperate

[PHONETIC] ree-KOO-puh-rayt

[DEFINITION] Bimari, injury, ya weakness ke baad slowly recover aur healthy hona.

[NARRATIVE] Imagine ek boxer fight ke baad hospital bed par tha. Doctor bola:

“Recover… eight days lagenge.”

8 days baad woh slowly healthy aur strong ho gaya.

Yahan “Recover eight” sunne mein “Recuperate” jaisa lagta hai, aur recovery process directly meaning show karti hai.

[STORY] Zane trekking trip se wapas aaya toh completely exhausted tha. Uska body pain kar raha tha aur woh properly walk bhi nahi kar paa raha tha. Doctor ne bola ki usko kuch din proper rest lena padega to **Recuperate**.

Pehle do din Zane sirf bed par movies dekhta raha. Kritika uske liye soup aur fruits laati thi. Dheere dheere uski energy wapas aane lagi.

Ek week baad Zane balcony mein baith kar books padh raha tha. Dipti ne notice kiya ki woh pehle se kaafi better lag raha hai. Usne bola, “Looks like you’re finally starting to **Recuperate**.”

Zane hasi aur bola, “Yeah, ab body normal feel ho rahi hai.”

Few days later woh phir morning walks par jaana start kar diya.

[USAGE]

[Bimari ke baad recover hona ho | “He stayed home to recuperate after the fever.”]

[Injury ke baad strength wapas aana | “The athlete needed time to recuperate.”]

[Exhaustion ke baad rest lena | “She took a vacation to recuperate.”]

[Mental stress ke baad recover hona | “He went to the hills to recuperate peacefully.”]

[Kisi operation ke baad healing process | “It took months for him to recuperate fully.”]
