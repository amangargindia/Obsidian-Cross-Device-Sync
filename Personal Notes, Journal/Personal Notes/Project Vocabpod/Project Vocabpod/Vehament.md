[WORD] Vehement

[PHONETIC] VEE-uh-ment

[DEFINITION] Bahut forcefully, emotionally, ya aggressively apni baat express karna.

[NARRATIVE] Simple discussion chal rahi thi. Suddenly ek banda bahut emotional ho gaya. Table par haath maara, loudly argue karne laga, aur nonstop shout kar raha tha.

Sab bole:

“BRO BECAME VERY MENTAL!”

Yahan “Very mental” sunne mein “Vehement” jaisa lagta hai, aur uska intense emotional reaction directly meaning show karta hai.

[STORY] Dex aur Kritika café mein startup ideas discuss kar rahe the. Conversation normal chal rahi thi until ek businessman next table par loudly argue karne laga. Woh apne partner se itne strong tone mein baat kar raha tha ki poora café uski taraf dekhne laga.

Usne table par coffee cup zor se rakha aur bola ki woh deal kabhi accept nahi karega. Uska expression aur voice dono bahut **Vehement** the.

Kritika slowly boli, “Bro is extremely **Vehement** about this topic.”

Manager aaya aur unko calm down karne ki koshish ki, but businessman phir bhi full emotion aur force ke saath argue karta raha. Finally uska partner chup ho gaya because the argument had become too **Vehement**.

Baad mein Dex bola, “Aaj pehli baar real life mein itna **Vehement** argument dekha.”

[USAGE]

[Kisi argument mein bahut forcefully bolna ho | “She was vehemently against the decision.”]

[Kisi opinion ko strong emotion ke saath express karna ho | “He gave a vehement response.”]

[Kisi protest ya objection mein intensity ho | “The crowd made a vehement protest.”]

[Kisi debate mein aggressive emotional tone ho | “They had a vehement argument.”]

[Kisi cheez ko strongly deny karna ho | “He issued a vehement denial.”]
