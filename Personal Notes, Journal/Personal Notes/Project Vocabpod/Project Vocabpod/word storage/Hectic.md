[WORD] Hectic

[PHONETIC] hek-tik

[DEFINITION] Bahut zyada busy, chaotic, stressful aur idhar-udhar bhaagne waali situation ko **hectic** bolte hain.

[NARRATIVE] Imagine ek office mein boss zor se chillata hai — “HECK!! Tick-tick-tick! Jaldi karo!” Clock bahut fast chal rahi hai, papers udd rahe hain, sab log panic mein bhaag rahe hain. Pura scene crazy aur overloaded hai. “HECK + tick-tick” directly yaad dilata hai ki situation bahut busy aur chaotic hai — yani **hectic**.

[STORY]  
Indore ki ek startup company mein Darpan ka pehla din tha. Subah se hi office ka atmosphere bahut **hectic** tha. Printer jam ho gaya tha, internet baar-baar disconnect ho raha tha, aur clients continuously calls kar rahe the.

Dex ek haath se coffee pee raha tha aur doosre haath se presentation bana raha tha. Kritika loudly bol rahi thi, “Yaar aaj ka din itna **hectic** kyun hai?”

Tabhi boss andar aaya aur bola, “Meeting 10 minute mein prep honi chahiye!”

Sab aur bhi zyada panic mein aa gaye. Deepmaala files leke ek desk se doosri desk bhaag rahi thi. Zane accidentally coffee gira deta hai important documents par. Pura office ekdum movie jaisa **hectic** lag raha tha.

Shaam tak sab log thak chuke the. Darpan ne chair par baithte hue bola:

“Main samajh gaya… startup life matlab permanent **hectic** mode.”

[QUIZ_EXP] “Hectic” ka matlab hota hai bahut busy, chaotic aur stressful situation. Isliye option 3 correct hai. “Relaxing” aur “Peaceful” iska opposite feel dete hain, aur “Silent” sirf awaaz na hone ko batata hai — busy chaos ko nahi.
