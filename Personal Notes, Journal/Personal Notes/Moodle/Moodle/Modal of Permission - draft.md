Modal verb of permission are used to
- Ask for permission
- Give permission
- Deny permission

Modal verbs of permissions are graded based on the politeness level.
- You can be extremely polite
- Less polite
- Least polite

There are three modal verbs of permission
- Can 
- Could 
- May

It depends on whether or not you can use a modal to ask, give, or deny permission

| Modal verb | Can be used to ask permission? | Can be used to deny the permission? | Can be used to allow permission? | Politeness |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Can        | Yes                            | Yes                                 | Yes                              | Least      |
| Could      | Yes                            | No                                  | No                               | Middle     |
| May        | Yes                            | Yes                                 | Yes                              | Least      |


---
The general structure for a permission request is:  
\(\text{Modal}+\text{Subject}+\text{Base\ Verb}\)
