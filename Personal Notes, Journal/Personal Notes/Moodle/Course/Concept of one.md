Articles show that there is One of something.
Cat vs A cat - you are specifying that there is one of something.
- “I saw **an elephant** at the zoo.” → One elephant among possibly many.
- - “She bought **a dress**.” → Just one dress from countless options.
"The idea here is **singularity within a group**. The noun is countable and singular, and the indefinite article highlights that it’s **only one representative** of its category."

Specificity:
When you mention something for the first time, you mention a, an etc.
When you refer to it later on, you call it by the.
"I saw an elephant at the zoo. The elephant was really big."
